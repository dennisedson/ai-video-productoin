# Dennis's voice (HubSpot Developers)

What a script for Dennis should sound like. Seeded on 2026-11-24 from two finished videos (agents-2 and agentcli);
the Results phase proposes changes after each video by comparing the script with what he actually said. Only
Dennis's own videos count here; a guest presenter's video is not his voice.

## How he opens
- Casual, straight in: "Hey y'all, Dennis Edson here, builder advocate at HubSpot, and we are going to be talking
  about ..." or a hook that starts from the last video: "I just published a video walking through ..."
- Names the series early: "how HubSpot is going headless".
- Credits colleagues by role and points to the links: "an exercise that my colleague, also a builder advocate here
  at HubSpot, has developed and put on GitHub, which I will have links to in the description below."
- A turn into the work: "Let's roll up our sleeves and get to work." / "But let's get started."

## How he talks through a demo
- First person, present tense, narrating as he goes: "I'm going to open up Claude", "we're gonna do daily at this
  odd time. Hit create."
- Tool-agnostic asides: "You can use ChatGPT or whatever agent you happen to be using, but I am using Claude right now."
- Plain reasons, with the cost or effort in mind: "That round trip is burning your credits." "This is yours to keep,
  which is a nice handy thing and will save you some money in the end."
- Light humour about real life: "I already said that I'm not gonna work on Saturday. It's just not gonna happen. Sorry."

## How he signs off
- "Got questions? Put them down below. Let's talk about it. Until next time. Bye bye."
- Or points to the next video: "I will be following up with another video soon on the agent CLI. Until then, bye bye."

## Never
- Claims that aren't true or that the docs don't back ("you never have to run an agent again" was cut at B).
- Corporate phrasing or hype words; he says "handy", "nice", "great for light tasks".
- Long read-aloud passages: he reads the hook, the setup and the sign-off, and ad-libs the demos.

## For the script
- Read-aloud lines short enough for a teleprompter line or two each.
- Leave room for asides in the demo beats instead of scripting them.
- Mark where a result must stay on screen for two seconds.
